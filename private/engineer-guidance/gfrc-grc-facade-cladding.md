## 1. System Intent
The GFRC/GRC façade cladding system creates architectural exterior surfaces with
custom forms, profiles, textures, and façade treatments — panel rhythm, cornice
lines, opening surrounds, and edge expression — engineered to the approved
architectural design.

## 2. Suitable Applications
Commercial · Hospitality · Residential · Heritage · Institutional · Mixed Use.

## 3. Design Guidance Areas
- **Panel Layout** — module sizing, joint spacing, horizontal/vertical banding.
- **Opening Surround** — reveal depth, surround width, shadow line.
- **Edge & Corner** — quoin treatment, return panels, corner expression.
- **Finish Selection** — finish direction for context, exposure, and design intent.
- **Project Coordination** — panel joint sizing and weathering considerations,
  coordinated with the approved architectural drawings.

## 4. Finish Directions (examples)
Smooth · Sandblasted · Textured · Stone-Like · Custom Colour · Heritage-Inspired.
Custom colour matching and special surface textures are available subject to
project specification. (Directions only — final finishes confirmed per project.)

## 5. Information Required for Technical Review
- Architectural drawings and elevations
- Approximate areas and quantities
- Design intent images or references
- Preferred finish direction
- Project location
- Project timeline
- Consultant / client requirements
- Special design considerations

## 6. Important Note
This guide is for **design understanding and early coordination only**. Final
fixing, structural calculations, and project-specific connections are issued only
through **approved shop drawings and project-specific engineering submissions**.
